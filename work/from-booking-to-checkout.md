I built Guest Pass so customers could find a nearby salon, see its wait time and book a service with a stylist. On the salon side, I developed Super Salon’s scheduling and checkout software and programmed the payment terminals and barcode scanners. Around 30,000 salons used Super Salon, handling hundreds of millions of dollars in transactions each year.
