## Two ways into the site

A visitor who knew which Aleve product they wanted could go straight to Products or Where to Buy. Someone browsing by topic could start with the featured customer story. On the archived homepage, both routes are visible alongside Marilyn’s account of getting back to her garden.

My responsibility at Ciber was leading development of the brand website. The homepage and the individual story pages shared the same product and topic navigation. On the story page, readers could follow the featured product link or choose another person’s account without retracing their steps through the homepage.
