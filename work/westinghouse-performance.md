## My role in the release

I worked at Westinghouse through Sogeti / Capgemini as a QA Manager and SDET. My broader remit covered automation and performance testing across 13 projects. For the website release, my responsibility was to find where the infrastructure struggled under load and use those findings to guide improvements.

## From load tests to a configuration change

Performance testing exposed bottlenecks. I used the test results to tune the Elasticsearch configuration. After the changes, Elasticsearch handled three times the throughput of its earlier configuration.
